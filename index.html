Questions we get a lot

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. We set everything up and show you how to use it. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.

Will customers know they’re talking to AI?

You decide. Most businesses tell customers it’s an assistant, and customers like the fast replies. A real person can always take over.

Can it reply in Nepali?

Yes: Nepali script, English and Romanized Nepali. We test it on your real customer questions before launch, and you can review the replies.

Do I keep my current WhatsApp number?

Yes. We connect to your business number through WhatsApp Business.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It only answers from information you approve (menu, prices, hours). Anything it’s unsure about gets handed to you.

How long does setup take?

It depends on what you need. We’ll give you a clear timeline before we start.
