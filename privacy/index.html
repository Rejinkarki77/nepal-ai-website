Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This page explains what information this website collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and plain.

Who we are

This website belongs to a small company in Nepal that sets up WhatsApp and AI automation for local businesses. “We” means that company. To ask a question or make a request about your information, use the contact details in the contact section of our home page.

What we collect and why

The contact form

If you use the form, we collect your name, your business name and type, and what you would like to automate. We save this in our database (hosted by Supabase in Mumbai, India) so we can reply to you about a free demo. If the form opens WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to that message.

The AI assistant

When you type or speak to the assistant, your words are sent to our server and to Cloudflare’s AI service, which turns speech into text and writes the reply. We do not save voice recordings, and we do not save the conversation. If you agree to a free demo and give us your name and a phone number, WhatsApp number or email, we save those details the same way as the contact form. The assistant is an AI, not a person. Please do not share passwords or card numbers with it.

Visit counts

We count visits to our pages so we can see what is useful. Each count records only the page address, the website that sent you (if any), your country, and whether you used a phone or a computer. It does not use cookies, and we do not store your IP address or any way to identify you. If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, we do not count your visit.

Hosting

Our website is delivered by Cloudflare. To do that and to keep the site secure, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address.

Fonts

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google, and Google can see your IP address when it does.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. It keeps two small notes in your browser tab (not on our servers): that you accepted the assistant’s notice, and that this page was already counted. They disappear when you close the tab.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show advertising or use tracking cookies.
  • We do not ask for passwords, card numbers or ID numbers.

How long we keep information

We keep the details you give us for as long as we need them to follow up with you and run our business. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it.
  • Use the contact form or a message instead of the AI assistant if you prefer.
  • Turn on “Do Not Track” in your browser to stay out of our visit counts.

Security

Information you send is carried over HTTPS and stored in a protected database. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
